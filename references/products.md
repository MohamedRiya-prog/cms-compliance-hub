## BDD — Backdraft Damper

**Ratings:**
- Maximum Velocity: 2,600 fpm (13.2 m/s)
- Maximum Static Pressure Rating: 9.43 in.wg. (2,347 Pa)
- Temperature: -40°F to 200°F (-40°C to 93°C)
- Leakage: class I (6 cfm/ft² @ 1 in.wg).
- Test Standard: AMCA 500D
- Test Laboratory: Intertek
- AMCA seal: Tested as per AMCA 500D for air performance and leakage at Intertek Laboratories (Without ACMA seal/sticker).
- Pressure Drop: 75Pa at 1672FPM face velocity for (610 x 610) size.

**Construction:**
- Frame: 1.2mm galvanized steel standard. Options: 1.6mm, 2.0mm, 2.4mm, 3.0mm galvanized steel; 1.2mm aluminium; 1.2mm stainless steel to 3.0mm stainless steel
- Double Skin Frame (optional) : Comply (When specified sleeve will become mandatory, thickness shall be same as frame or higher in gauge)
- Blades: 0.8mm extruded aluminium, max 100mm wide, foam gasket seal (adhesive). Options: 0.8mm galvanized steel; 0.8mm stainless steel; 1.2mm aluminium sheet (when specified, shaft: 10 x 10mm square to 8mm round galvanized steel)
- Blade style: Single piece, end pivot (offset pivot), parallel action, gravity operated
- Rattle-free construction; 90-degree blade stop standard
- Axle: 8mm aluminium pipe with 6mm threaded rod (standard with 0.8mm blades). With 1.2mm aluminium blade: galvanized steel axle
- Axle (optional): Brass shaft (when specified blade will be 0.8mm thick extruded aluminium)
- Blade seal: Foam gasket adhesive type (standard); Options: Neoprene adhesive type.
- Jamb seal: Without jamb seal.
- Bearings: PVC/Nylon standard; brass or stainless steel optional
- Tie bar / Linkage: Aluminium standard; Galvanized steel (when specified, blade material as galvanized steel); Stainless steel (when specified, blade material as stainless steel).
- Tie bar / Linkage (optional): Concealed type (when specified blade will be 1.2mm thick aluminium)  
- Mounting: Horizontal or vertical
- Stainless steel dampers - Contractor to specify wherever required.

**Accessories:**
- Sleeve: Optional — contractor to specify. Gauges: 1.2mm, 1.6mm, 2.0mm. Length: to suit application
- Bird screen: Optional — contractor to specify. Galvanized steel (1.0mm, 6x6mm) or aluminium (8x10mm)

## EVFD — Curtain Type Fire Damper

**Models:** EVFD-10-A/B/C, EVFD-10D-A/B/C, EVFD-30-A/B/C, EVFD-30D-A/B/C
- 10 series: 1.5-hour UL 555 fire rated
- 30 series: 3-hour UL 555 fire rated
- Style A: Blades in airstream; Style B: Blades out of airstream; Style C: Blades out + 100% free area

**STATIC vs DYNAMIC — MODEL SELECTION (applies to all compliance decisions):**
- EVFD-10 / EVFD-30 (no D suffix): Static rated — closes after system shutdown, when airflow has stopped. DEFAULT model to propose.
- EVFD-10D / EVFD-30D (D suffix): Dynamic rated — closes while the system is operating, against live airflow. Propose ONLY when spec explicitly requires dynamic rating or closure against airflow.
- Closure springs are standard on ALL models (standard and D-series). Spring presence does NOT indicate dynamic rating.

**Ratings — EVFD-10 / EVFD-30 (Static, default):**
- Maximum Rated Pressure: 4 in.wg. (1.0 kPa)
- Rated for static closure (system off, no airflow)
- UL 555 listed; Civil Defence approved

**Ratings — EVFD-10D / EVFD-30D (Dynamic, specify only when required):**
- Maximum Rated Pressure: 4 in.wg. (1.0 kPa)
- Maximum Dynamic Closure Velocity: 2,000 fpm standard; 4,000 fpm in limited configurations
- UL 555 dynamic rated; Civil Defence approved

**Construction (all models):**
- Frame: 22 gauge (0.85mm) galvanized steel
- Frame (optional): 22 gauge (0.85mm) Stainless 304 Steel.
- Blades: 24 gauge (0.70mm) galvanized steel, curtain style
- Blades (optional): 24 gauge (0.70mm) stainless 304 steel, curtain style
- Sleeve: Factory installed. Gauges: 20 (1.0mm), 18 (1.2mm), 16 (1.6mm), 14 (2.0mm), 10 (3.5mm). Length: 300mm standard
- Closure springs: Stainless steel, standard on ALL models (standard and D-series)
- Fusible link: 165°F (74°C), replaceable;
- Fusible link (optional): 212°F (100°C)
- Mounting: Vertical and horizontal (10-series); Vertical only (30-series)
- Limit switch (micro switch): Yes (optional)
